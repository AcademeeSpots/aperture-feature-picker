How the whole system works

Your manual follows each investment from start to finish. Investor money comes in, the team finds and tests deals, closes them, runs the properties, manages and replaces the debt, reports to investors, eventually sells or exits, and the monitoring step feeds lessons back into every stage.

  6. Refinancemanage & replace debt→
  7. Reportinvestors & regulators→
  8. Exitsell, 1031 or 721→
  9. Monitortest & feed back ↺

Governance rules (who approves what, conflicts, record-keeping) sit over every stage.

The four economic engines

The manual keeps four sources of value separate, so one is never passed off as another (for example, refinance cash presented as operating success).

1Property operations

Real income gains from rent, occupancy, expenses and light improvements.

2Financing & refinancing

What debt adds or costs: bridge loans, long-term loans, rate caps, cash-outs.

3Tax-structured & capital formation

How investor money arrives and leaves: 1031/DST, 721/REIT, subscriptions and redemptions.

4Sponsor economics

Fees, promote, related-party services and conflicts of interest.

Plain-English glossary of terms used below
SOP
Standard Operating Procedure: a written “how we do this” rulebook for one process. Each card lists the SOP IDs it draws on.
IC
Investment Committee: the group that approves major decisions.
LOI
Letter of intent: the first written offer to buy a property.
Bridge loan / takeout
A short-term loan used to buy and improve a property, and the long-term loan that later replaces it.
Rate cap
A contract that limits how high a floating interest rate can go.
Covenant
A promise in a loan agreement, such as keeping income above a set multiple of loan payments.
NOI
Net operating income: rent and other income minus operating costs.
NAV
Net asset value: what the fund's holdings are worth, in total or per share.
Sources & uses
Where the money for a deal comes from and where it goes at closing.
1031 / DST
A tax-deferred property exchange, and a Delaware Statutory Trust, one way investors hold property interests.
721 / UPREIT
Contributing property interests into a REIT's operating partnership in exchange for units.
Related party
A person or company connected to the sponsor, such as an affiliated property manager.
Evidence label
A tag on each claim: Confirmed, Partially Supported, Management / Sponsor Claim, Industry Practice, Analytical Inference, or Unverified.
Effort S / M / L
Our rough sense of build size: Small, Medium or Large. These are estimates, not quotes.

Important notes. Effort sizes and timelines are rough estimates for planning only. They are not quotes or commitments, and they would be firmed up after a short discovery phase and depend on data access and decision turnaround.

The underlying control documents are internal control templates. Neither they nor this page are legal, tax, securities, accounting or investment advice. Matters flagged for professional review still need qualified counsel and advisors.

Dollar and percentage thresholds in the underlying SOPs (for example approval limits) are still placeholders, to be set by your accountable executives. Nothing on this page states facts about any fund's performance or claims that any feature already exists.
